South Africa's Transnet Returns to Profit After Four Years

September 10, 2026 · Africa Today Business Desk
🚆 🚢

South Africa's Freight & Logistics Sector

South Africa's state-owned logistics company Transnet reported a profit of 4.6 billion rand for the financial year ending March 2026.

The result marks the company's first annual profit in four years, according to Reuters.

Key figures

Profit: 4.6 billion rand

Revenue: 88.6 billion rand

Revenue growth: 7.1%

Transnet said the improvement was mainly supported by the sale of its Pier 2 terminal and increased volumes in rail and pipeline freight.

The company has faced years of equipment shortages and maintenance backlogs that affected freight movement and weighed on South Africa's economy.

Reuters reported that government guarantees have also helped strengthen Transnet's liquidity and reduce the risk of a debt default.

The latest results suggest that efforts to improve the company's operations are beginning to produce stronger financial results.

Source: Reuters
