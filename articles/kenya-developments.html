Kenya Expected to Receive About $400 Million in Emergency World Bank Financing

September 10, 2026 · Africa Today News Desk
🇰🇪

Kenya is expected to receive approximately $400 million in emergency financing from the World Bank within six weeks, according to reporting by Reuters.

The financing is being prepared through the World Bank's Rapid Response Option, an emergency mechanism designed to help countries respond more quickly to major crises.

The planned support comes as Kenya faces several economic and environmental pressures, including concerns linked to regional health risks, El Niño disruptions and elevated energy prices.

According to the Reuters report, the funding could help reduce pressure on sectors including healthcare, agriculture and water resources while supporting the wider economy.

The final amount will depend on the country's undisbursed World Bank financing balance when the emergency response arrangements are completed.
